The Oppo initially reported about 10,240 MiB free browser quota, no persistent-storage grant, camera permission prompt, browser mode and a save-picker API. Those are runtime capability observations, not guaranteed hardware disk capacity or successful native picker tests. The camera was subsequently opened and its track stopped on the actual handset; no optical scan or frame artifact was recorded. It had direct ICE connectivity over Wi-Fi; USB forwarded signaling and Chrome debugging only. Test sources were programmatically selected synthetic files. Real Android downloads were pulled by exact test filename for independent hash comparison. The screen-lock/app-switch cases began paused; active-hidden pausing is covered by browser lifecycle injection. Offline/online was injected on the handset's test target, not physical Wi-Fi roaming.

Unknown quota permits at most 64 MiB per new offer with acknowledgement. Known quota needs 10%/4 MiB headroom. Offers from 300 MiB show an extra warning. These are application policy thresholds; the measured success size is not an upper bound. No 10 GB iPhone, unlimited transfer, eviction-proof storage or background WebRTC claim is made.
